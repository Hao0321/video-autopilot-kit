# 3D character audit (provisional)

Read-only; owner: [R&D](../../../run-benchmark-driven-rd/references/topics/character-3d-workflow.md).
Resume: [retopology audit](character-retopology-audit.md).

[Helper](../../../game-mesh-repair/SKILL.md): audit global UV, fresh skin roles
and shader-color controls, not fixture PASS.

- `cleanup.character.evidence`: compare source/candidate topology, UV, skin/morphs and rest skeleton. Reject stale/missing hashes or shell-only success. Export cannot prove engine bone/morph/material/runtime readiness.
- `cleanup.character.texture`: separate UV validity, coverage and art. Test overlaps/degeneracy, stale mesh/camera, wrong-size/missing views and occlusion. Unwrap/generation/projection differ; no invented Blender image API.
- `cleanup.character.uv-storage`: audit stored/reloaded UVs, not double-precision plans. Require current-hash valid-copy and planned-pass/float32-fail controls; structure is not paintability. Evidence: character-uv-storage-20260912.
- `cleanup.character.uv-fragmentation`: compare native UV-chart count/layout with true geometry-connected components and semantic regions; a strand can be one component yet many unusable UV scraps. Require the actual chart image plus per-mesh overlap/degeneracy before accepting a repaint or bake plan. Do not equate all disconnected hair/ornament shells with debris.
- `cleanup.character.hair-uv-pilot`: reject a semantic hair UV candidate even when chart count improves if the saved atlas contains degenerate triangles, especially concentrated in a large cap/strand component. A further simple region split that worsens collapse is negative evidence, not an invitation to weaken limits. Demand revised structural cuts/retopology and fresh native bake evidence before promotion.
- `cleanup.character.hair-orientability`: inspect edge-connected components, boundary loops and global face-orientation consistency separately from vertex connectivity and fan-edge count. A zero-fan shell can be non-orientable; a solver swap or a few local winding seams cannot be promoted without stored-UV and native chart readback. Treat a topology preflight PASS as diagnosis only, not a UV/retopo PASS.
- `cleanup.character.component-hybrid-uv`: verify source and draft quality per identical edge-connected surface, record precisely which faces/corners were reused or locally patched, then cold-audit the **combined** atlas. Flag a numerically valid atlas with excessive islands, poor occupied area or unreadable garment/hair panels. Source-to-new UV color rebake proves mapping continuity, not regenerated art. A collapsed internal wedge may be dissolved only with unchanged surviving positions/weights/materials and neutral/posed evaluated comparison; this is not a global polygon-reduction mandate.
- `cleanup.character.triangle-chart-hair`: audit any xatlas/triangle-chart fallback as a dependency and a geometry migration, not a magical UV PASS. Verify output triangle-to-source ID mapping, quad-diagonal UV conflicts, exactly which quads were split, unchanged vertex positions/weights/materials and old UV readback. Check full-atlas exact overlap after packing: a library can return mostly clean charts but still leave local collisions. Reaudit after witness-only patch/repack and cold reload; separate numeric UV PASS and source-colour rebake from final artist-readable layout or new NPR art approval. Do not bundle the trial wheel or user model in the public skill by accident.
- `cleanup.character.hair-orientation-cut`: do not conflate zero fan edges, an orientable post-cut graph and outward/consistent **current** normals. Check the read-only contradiction-edge plan, vertex-duplication receipt, unchanged UV/weights/triangles and remaining local winding conflicts. A plan requiring extensive face flips is not an automatic fix; demand front/back culling and native normal/animation art review before promoting it.
- `cleanup.character.hair-two-sided`: even if an additional local cut reduces winding conflicts to zero and preserves every face corner/UV/weight, require a front/quarter/back **one-sided culling** negative control. A two-sided source-color render being pixel-identical does not mean Unreal one-sided hair survives; record lost strand coverage and defer any two-sided material cost/outline/lighting judgment to native engine review. Keep rear-scalp coverage as a separate defect.
- `cleanup.character.render-uv`: check Blender editing/bake UV and `active_render` separately. A source-colour bake can be correct while a review render uses the old UV and shows false black patches. Require explicit original-UV source shader, new-UV bake target, new render-UV selection and cold native captures before rejecting or approving the bake.
- `cleanup.character.ai-atlas`: inspect returned generated-image dimensions, source/candidate UV-island mask registration, missing/leaking texels, motif continuity and native on-mesh front/quarter/back. Do not promote a sharper-looking 1254-square candidate into a 2K atlas, nor quietly substitute an Imagegen concept image for a pixel-registered NPR base color. Keep failures and prompts as reproducible review evidence.
- `cleanup.character.ai-detail-layer`: when a rejected whole-atlas edit is replaced by a generated fabric-detail swatch, verify that it is explicitly a material layer, not falsely a new full-base atlas. Check tile border error, source motif retention and semantic masking; a brightness-only ivory mask can spill patterns onto pale skin in a shared mesh/material. Require skin/cloth close-ups, full-frame comparison and hash-bound target-resolution rebake if exported. Keep partial cloth improvements and final full-character material acceptance separate.
- `cleanup.character.part-registration`: compare assembled head/hair/body **world bounds** to the original registered asset, not just equality of object matrices between UV derivatives. Normalized unit-height local vertices can create a fivefold head or oversized hair if the original scale/translation is lost; flag this as assembly-coordinate failure unless the mesh geometry itself changed. A corrected static view remains separate from hair coverage, weights and UE integration.
- `cleanup.character.ornament-visibility`: audit a claimed AI repair of blurry gold/decals by matched close-up and motif-contour comparison. A slight luminance/grain change or small global pixel delta is not evidence that the original unreadable ornament was reconstructed; retain the generated microtile as a rejected hypothesis and keep the registered atlas as fallback.
- `cleanup.character.scope-override`: reconcile the latest user density instruction with old low-poly plans. If polygon count is explicitly accepted, flag new decimation as out of scope; continue defect, UV, texel and texture work without claiming downstream rig/Unreal integration on a different topology.
- `cleanup.character.closure`: retain rights/privacy and physics/runtime gaps. Thresholds/receipts stay local; pretty/synthetic fixtures do not prove production quality.
- `cleanup.character.npr`: audit [normals](../../../run-benchmark-driven-rd/references/topics/character-npr-normals.md), import/LOD recompute, negative controls, outline seams, morph/skin parity and native light/motion. Build is not beauty.
- `cleanup.character.material-persist`: on current UE asset, inspect master→instance parent/texture, skeletal mesh slot interfaces, preview actor overrides and cold-reopened values separately. Save-success or source-tone screenshots cannot establish persisted slot bindings or finished NPR; a slot array mutated through returned structs may revert on reload in UE 4.27 Python.
- `cleanup.character.repair`: audit [repair](../../../run-benchmark-driven-rd/references/topics/character-mesh-repair.md): boundaries may be interfaces; zero fans is not watertight. Preserve source/corners/UV; require opening negatives, matched captures/reload, split correspondence and seam-weight/deformation checks. Unresolved holes/intersections stay REVIEW.
- `cleanup.character.rig-guide`: if an external auto-rig/VRM route is proposed, demand the actual output format, cost/upload authority, rest-pose hierarchy, weight provenance and source hashes. A Tripo-style rig guide is not transferred hand-painted weights, and a VRM humanoid is not an Unreal skeleton by name. Reject claims that a whole-wig rigid head binding supplies hair sway, or that Blender pose tests establish UE runtime physics. Check unweighted vertices, normalization, >4-influence mobile exceptions, masks at joints/garments, pose return and native engine readback on the **current** asset.
